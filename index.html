Raspberry Pi 5

The standard bearer of educational and maker computing. Featuring a custom 2.4 GHz quad-core Cortex-A76 processor, RP1 I/O controller, PCIe 2.0 expansion ribbon, and the most mature ARM Linux distribution on the planet.

Architecture 4x A76 @ 2.4 GHz
RAM Capacity 1GB - 16GB LPDDR4X
Wired LAN 1x Gigabit Ethernet
Storage MicroSD + PCIe 2.0 FPC
Video Out Dual Micro-HDMI 4K60
Community #1 Global Ecosystem
Best for: Education, STEM, Home Assistant, Lightweight Nodes
29+ TOPS Intelligent Edge Qualcomm Snapdragon 8cx Gen 3

Radxa Dragon Q8B

The next generation of high-performance edge computing. Powered by a 5nm Qualcomm Snapdragon 8cx Gen 3 octa-core chip, built-in 29+ TOPS Hexagon NPU, Dual 2.5GbE ports, and Dual native M.2 NVMe SSD slots right on the PCB.

Architecture 8-Core Kryo (4x 3.0GHz)
AI NPU 29+ TOPS Onboard
Wired LAN Dual 2.5GbE (5 Gbps)
Storage Dual M.2 NVMe (PCIe 3.0)
Displays Triple 4K120 (HDMI 2.1)
Memory Bus 128-bit (8-Channel)
Best for: Local LLMs, 2.5G Routers, TrueNAS, Workstations
